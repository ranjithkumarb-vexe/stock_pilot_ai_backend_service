**Behaviour worth knowing**
- Design deltas are intentional: universe = 16 design tickers + `V` (TASKS asks for >= 17); `SPY` is the benchmark row (`in_universe=false`); CIKs are stored zero-padded to 10 digits. Prices carry `prices_daily.source='simulated'`; the seed never touches vendor rows.
- Seeded personas are built from REAL fills: an opening buy per holding is solved so the average-cost replay gives the design's quantity and cost basis to 4 dp, then the design's recent orders follow; `portfolios.starting_cash` is whatever makes the design cash come out (Priya 92,978.60, Alex 84,240.89; a sub-cent `adjustment` ledger row absorbs rounding). Equity snapshots (120 sessions) are calibrated so ytd/vol/beta equal the design stats exactly and drawdowns within ~0.1; they are NOT reconstructed from historical positions.
- Performance `turnover_pct`, `win_rate_pct`, `realized_pnl` are computed from fills and therefore differ from the design constants.
- Rule semantics: rule breaches on a BUY are `blocked` in preview and rejected with `rule_violation` unless `override: true` (logged as a `rule_override` event, never loosens a rule); trades/week is a Note for sells. No-shorting, cash (incl. slippage buffer + fee), whole shares, stale quotes (market orders only), limit price are hard failures. Queued buys are re-checked for cash at the open (rejected with `insufficient_cash` otherwise); there is no cash reservation across queued orders.
- `GET /profile` recomputes stream C's features when they are older than 10 min (so it writes), which replaces the seed's hand-written `revealed_tier`/`bias:*` with computed values.
